## 6. Description (paste as-is)
```
Social media manager planner app for freelancers: client brief, content calendar, approval tracker and monthly reports in one offline file. Pay once. No subscription. No login.

Every client yes is saved against the exact caption version. Edit an approved post and the version goes up and the approval resets, so you always know what the client actually said yes to.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
WHO IT'S FOR
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
• Freelance social media managers with 1 to a few clients
• Anyone tired of approvals lost in DMs, emails and chat threads
• Anyone done paying a monthly fee to keep a content plan

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
WHAT IT DOES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
• OVERVIEW: one next step, posts coming up, approvals waiting
• CONTENT BOARD: six stages (Draft → Checks → Needs approval → Ready → Scheduled → Live), board or list, search, filters, CSV export
• CONTENT CALENDAR: month view coloured by stage; click a date to add a post
• APPROVAL TRACKER: facts and rights checks come first; records approver, date, channel and version
• CLIENT BRIEF: 10 guided fields with a progress bar
• ASSETS: visual library for client photos (4 sample photos included)
• REPORTS: reach, interactions and engagement rate per post; print or save as an A4 PDF
• GUIDE & TEMPLATES: 5-minute practice workflow and the client documents
• SETTINGS: your studio name, light/dark mode, CSV export, JSON backup and restore

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
WHAT YOU GET (one ZIP)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
1. Brief-to-Approved.html: the planner app
2. Start Here guide (PDF, 2 pages, with your private online link for phone and tablet)
3. Client Documents (PDF): 6 A4 layouts: Welcome Packet, Client Brief, Strategy Snapshot, Content Review, Monthly Report, Project Handoff
4. Editable Documents (HTML): type in your browser, then print or save
5. Visual Field Guide (PDF, 14 pages)
6. Spreadsheet Companion (XLSX, 5 tabs)
7. Licence (use for your business and client work)

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
HOW TO START
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
1. Download the ZIP from Etsy → Purchases and unzip it.
2. Double-click Brief-to-Approved.html. It opens in your browser.
3. Try the fictional demo client, then reset to an empty workspace and add your own.
4. On your phone: open the private online link in your Start Here PDF and add it to your home screen.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
GOOD TO KNOW
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
• Works in Chrome, Safari, Firefox and Edge on Mac, Windows, iPad and iPhone.
• No internet needed after download.
• Your data stays in your browser on that device. It does not sync between devices. Export a backup regularly; clearing browser data deletes it.
• It records approvals you received elsewhere (email, DM). It is not a shared client login portal.
• The XLSX needs Excel, Google Sheets or Numbers.
• Sample client, names and numbers are fictional.
• Digital download: nothing is shipped.
• Do not resell or share the files.

Questions? Message me on Etsy.
```
