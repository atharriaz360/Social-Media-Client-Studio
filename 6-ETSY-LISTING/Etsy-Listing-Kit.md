## 1. Market Research & Strategic Positioning

### Product Overview
* **Product Name**: brief to approved. — Client Studio (Edition 04 / v4.0)
* **Shop / Creator**: KaelLabs
* **Product Type**: Offline Single-File HTML Web Application + Client Document Suite (Digital Download)
* **Core Function**: A private, distraction-free content workspace for freelance Social Media Managers (SMMs) covering client briefing, editorial calendar, content drafting, versioned approvals, asset management, and monthly performance reporting.
* **Key Delivery Formats**: Standalone `.html` application (instant browser launch), A4 PDF templates, editable in-browser HTML documents, `.xlsx` spreadsheet companion, and quick-start field guide.

### Target Audience & Customer Personas
1. **The Overwhelmed Solo Freelance SMM**:
   * *Pain point*: Drowning in client feedback spread across WhatsApp, Slack, Instagram DMs, email threads, and Google Docs.
   * *Desire*: A single "calm" dashboard where each post has exactly one clear next step, and client approvals are verifiably locked down.
2. **The "Notion-Fatigued" Marketer**:
   * *Pain point*: Tired of paying monthly subscriptions, wrestling with broken relational databases, slow load times, and confused clients who mess up Notion layouts.
   * *Desire*: An instant, clean, bespoke tool with zero setup, zero formulas to fix, and no login barriers for them or their clients.
3. **The Subscription-Burned Freelancer**:
   * *Pain point*: SaaS tools (Metricool, Later, Planoly, ClickUp, Asana, Monday) eating \$20–\$60/month into slim freelance margins.
   * *Desire*: Pay once, download immediately, own forever with zero recurring fees.
4. **The Privacy-Conscious Professional**:
   * *Pain point*: Client intellectual property and sensitive unreleased campaign details stored on yet another third-party cloud server.
   * *Desire*: 100% offline, local-storage operation with one-click JSON backup and complete data sovereignty.

### Competitive Advantages & Core Value Hooks
| Competitor Category | Their Shortcomings | Client Studio v4.0 Advantage |
|---|---|---|
| **Notion Templates** | Slow loading, steep learning curve, fragile formulas, requires a Notion account. | **"No Notion Needed"**: Opens instantly in any browser as an independent app. |
| **SaaS Tools (ClickUp/Airtable/Later)** | Recurring monthly subscriptions (\$15–\$60/mo), complex permission setups. | **Pay Once, Own Forever**: Zero subscriptions, zero user seat fees. |
| **Google Sheets / Excel** | Clunky interface, no visual image board, poor phone experience, no version locking. | **Designer Interface**: Kanban board, stage-coloured calendar, visual asset gallery, mobile-responsive. |
| **Generic PDF Planners** | Static, non-interactive, requires handwriting or re-typing copy manually. | **Full Interactive App**: Real-time stage progression, automated reach calculations, one-click CSV export. |

### Signature "Killer Feature": Version-Locked Approval Resets
In freelance social media management, the biggest source of client friction is: *"Wait — they changed the caption after approving it!"* 
Client Studio solves this with a built-in strict versioning engine:
* Every post requires **Facts & Rights** verification checks before client approval can even unlock.
* When approved copy is edited by even a single character, the version number automatically advances (e.g., from v1 to v2) and **the previous approval status instantly resets**.
* The SMM maintains an unassailable audit trail (approver name, date, exact copy approved, and approval channel evidence).
