## Description

```
Social media planner app for freelance social media managers: content calendar, client brief, client approval tracker and monthly reports in one offline file. Instant digital download. Pay once. No subscription. No account.

Stop hunting through emails, DMs and spreadsheets to find what a client signed off on. Keep client briefs, post ideas, content stages, approval records and reports together.

WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT
- Every approval is saved against the exact content version the client approved.
- Edit an approved caption and the version goes up and the approval resets, so you can see what still needs client review.
- Facts and rights checks must be ticked before a post can be approved.

WHO IT'S FOR
- Freelance social media managers with one to a few clients
- Independent content and marketing freelancers
- Small creative studios that send content to clients for review
- Freelancers who want an approval workflow without another monthly subscription

INSIDE THE APP
- Overview: your next step, upcoming posts and pending approvals
- Content board: six stages (Draft, Checks, Needs Approval, Ready, Scheduled, Live), board or list view, search, filter and CSV export
- Content calendar: monthly view coloured by stage. Click a date to add a post
- Client approval tracker: who approved, when, through which channel and which version
- Client brief: 10 guided fields with a progress indicator
- Asset library: keep client visuals together. Four sample images included
- Monthly reports: enter reach and interactions, engagement rate calculates for you. Print or save as A4 PDF
- Settings: studio name, light and dark theme, CSV export, JSON backup and restore

WHAT YOU GET
One ZIP file containing:
1. Brief-to-Approved.html: the full app in one file
2. Start Here PDF (2 pages): setup steps plus a private online link for phone and tablet
3. Client Documents PDF: six A4 layouts (Welcome Packet, Client Brief, Strategy Snapshot, Content Review, Monthly Report, Project Handoff)
4. Editable Documents: HTML versions you can fill in and print
5. Visual Field Guide PDF (14 pages)
6. Spreadsheet Companion (XLSX, five tabs)
7. Licence for use in your own business

HOW TO START
1. Download the ZIP from Etsy Purchases and unzip it.
2. Open Brief-to-Approved.html in your browser.
3. Explore the fictional demo client, then choose Settings → Start empty workspace.
Phone or tablet: open the private link in the Start Here PDF and add it to your home screen.

GOOD TO KNOW
- Works offline after download. The phone link needs internet the first time.
- Your work saves in your browser on that device. Nothing is uploaded.
- It does not sync between devices. Your computer and phone are separate workspaces.
- Export a JSON backup regularly. Clearing browser data or private browsing can erase work.
- It does not publish or schedule posts to social media.
- The approval tracker logs approvals you receive by email or DM. It is not a shared client portal.
- The XLSX opens in Excel, Google Sheets or Numbers.
- Sample clients, names and numbers are fictional.

DIGITAL DOWNLOAD
No physical item ships. Licensed for use in your own business. Please do not share, resell or redistribute the original files. No lifetime updates are promised.

HOW IT'S MADE
Designed and built by me at KaelLabs. AI tools assisted with parts of the code, images and listing text.

MORE FROM KAELLABS
Invoice Tracker & Client CRM App: invoices, payments and client records

NEED HELP?
Message me on Etsy with your device and browser. I'm happy to help.
```
