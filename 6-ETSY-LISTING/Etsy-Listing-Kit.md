## Description

```
Social media planner app with a content calendar, client approval tracker and monthly reports: one offline file for social media managers. Move posts from Draft to Live and record every approval against the exact version approved.

One-time purchase. No subscription or account. Works in Chrome, Safari and Firefox on desktop, laptop, iPhone and phone.

WHAT'S INSIDE
- Overview: your next step, upcoming posts and pending approvals
- Content board: Draft, Checks, Needs Approval, Ready, Scheduled, Live. Board or list view, search, filter, duplicate or delete posts, CSV export
- Content calendar: monthly view coloured by stage. Click a date to add a post
- Client approval tracker: who approved, when, through which channel and which version
- Printable review packs with selected posts, images, captions, version numbers and review deadlines
- Approval history: see saved versions and recorded decisions. Editing an approved post clears its current approval
- Separate client workspaces: add, switch or remove clients, with one JSON backup for all
- Facts and rights checks must be ticked before a post can be approved
- Client brief: 10 guided fields with a progress indicator, plus team contacts
- JPG, PNG and WebP post attachments plus four sample images
- Monthly reports: enter reach and interactions, engagement rate calculates for you. Print or save as A4 PDF
- Light and dark theme, CSV export, JSON backup and restore

WHO IT'S FOR
- Personal social media projects, portfolio practice and anyone learning a content review workflow

WHAT YOU GET
Brief-to-Approved-Digital-Download.zip:
1. Brief-to-Approved.html: the full app in one file
2. Brief-to-Approved-Start-Here.pdf: 2-page setup guide with a private link for phone and tablet
3. Documents/Client-Documents.pdf: 6 A4 layouts (Welcome Packet, Client Brief, Strategy Snapshot, Content Review, Monthly Report, Project Handoff)
4. Documents/Editable-Documents.html: fill in and print in your browser
5. Documents/Visual-Field-Guide.pdf: 14 pages of workflows and checklists
6. Documents/Spreadsheet-Companion.xlsx: 5 tabs, opens in Excel, Google Sheets or Numbers
7. LICENSE.txt: Personal use only

HOW TO START
1. Download the ZIP from Etsy Purchases and unzip it.
2. Open Brief-to-Approved.html in your browser.
3. Explore the demo client, then choose Settings → Start empty workspace.
Phone or tablet: open the private link in the Start Here PDF and add it to your home screen.

YOUR DATA
- Works offline after download. The phone link needs internet the first time.
- Your work saves in your browser on that device. Nothing is uploaded. No account.
- Each device keeps its own data. Move all client workspaces with JSON backup and restore.
- Export a JSON backup regularly. Clearing browser data or private browsing can erase work.

GOOD TO KNOW
- Share a PDF review pack and record the reply yourself.
- It does not publish or schedule posts to social media.
- Sample clients, names and numbers are fictional.
- Personal use only. Please do not share, resell or redistribute the original files.
- Designed and built by me at KaelLabs. AI tools assisted with parts of the app code, text and images.

NEED HELP?
Message me on Etsy with your device and browser. I'm happy to help.

ALSO IN MY SHOP
More single-file planner apps in the KaelLabs shop.

Digital download. Nothing is shipped. Pay once.
```
