SETUP (1 minute)
1. Unzip the download.
2. Double-click Brief-to-Approved.html. It opens in your browser.
   Works offline. No account, no install, no subscription.
3. Phone: AirDrop / send the file to your phone and open it in your browser.

FIRST ACTION
The app opens with a fictional example client (Juniper Studio).
Look around, then Settings → "Start empty workspace" to begin with your own client.

BACKUP (important)
Your work is saved in this browser on this device only.
Settings → "Export JSON backup" after each session. Keep the file safe.
To restore or move devices: Settings → "Import backup".
Clearing browser data, private windows or a different browser will not show your work.

LIMITS
- One browser, one device at a time. No cloud sync, no sharing link.
- Approvals record a decision you already received. It does not send requests or verify identity.
- Reports describe numbers you enter. It does not connect to social platforms.
